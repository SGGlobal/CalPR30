# Skippy Constitution

Version: 1.0

Ratified: June 30, 2026

Status: Active

---

> "Architecture outlives technology."

Every decision made within Skippy should strengthen the architecture rather than optimize for any specific model, provider, or trend.

The architecture is permanent.

Implementations are temporary.

## Mission

Build an AI operating system that learns from experience, reasons transparently, and remains independent of any single model, provider, or tool.

---

# Core Principles

## 1. Skippy owns the architecture.

Everything else is replaceable.

Models.
Providers.
Tools.
Adapters.

Skippy remains the constant.

---

## 2. One responsibility per module.

Every subsystem has exactly one purpose.

---

## 3. The Brain coordinates.

The Brain never stores memory.

The Brain never performs reasoning.

The Brain delegates.

---

## 4. Hermes reasons.

Hermes transforms observations into understanding.

Hermes does not own memory.

Hermes does not control tools.

---

## 5. Memory remembers.

Memory stores experiences.

Memory retrieves experiences.

Memory never decides.

---

## 6. Learning improves.

Learning evaluates outcomes.

Learning adjusts confidence.

Learning discovers patterns.

---

## 7. Adapters connect external systems.

Adapters never contain business logic.

They translate external information into Signals.

---

## 8. Intelligence is model-agnostic.

Language models provide capability.

They do not define Skippy.

Skippy may use one model or many.

Changing models must never require changing the architecture.

---

## 9. Every decision must be explainable.

If Skippy cannot explain a decision...

It should not make it.

---

## 10. Intelligence emerges from experience.

Memory is more valuable than prompts.

Learning is more valuable than memorization.

Understanding is more valuable than prediction.

---

## 11. Local-first.

Cloud only when it provides clear value.

Privacy is the default.

---

## 12. Every subsystem must be testable.

If a subsystem cannot be tested independently...

It is too tightly coupled.

---

## 13. The user remains in control.

Skippy recommends.

Skippy explains.

Skippy automates only within the authority explicitly granted by the user.

Final responsibility remains with the user.

# Non-Goals

Skippy is not designed to:

- Depend on a single AI provider.
- Lock users into proprietary services.
- Replace human judgment.
- Optimize for benchmarks alone.
- Hide or fabricate reasoning.
- Sacrifice maintainability for short-term speed.

---

# Long-Term Vision

Skippy is not an assistant.

Skippy is an AI operating system.

Every application—QLA, RimWorld, Albion, SGGlobal—is an adapter that extends the platform without changing its architecture.


"The quality of Skippy's intelligence is measured not only by the answers it gives, but by the questions it learns to ask."

Skippy does not optimize for answers.

Skippy optimizes for understanding.

---

# Oath

Every contribution to Skippy should make the system:

- Easier to understand.
- Easier to test.
- Easier to extend.
- Easier to trust.

If a contribution weakens those goals...

It should be redesigned.
